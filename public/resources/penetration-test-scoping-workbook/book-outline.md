# Penetration Test Scoping Workbook Outline

Target: 12,000 words plus worksheets, approximately 45-55 US Letter pages.

Audience: SaaS founders, CTOs, engineering managers, product security teams, compliance leads, and procurement teams preparing an authorized third-party penetration test.

Author: Sthuthi Securities, a Sthuthi Technologies, LLC product.

## Front matter

- Cover and title page
- Copyright, use terms, and security/legal disclaimer
- How to use the workbook
- Table of contents

## Part I Define the assessment

1. Start with the decision the test must support - 700 words
   - Buyer trigger, intended audience, deadline, required proof, success test
   - Worksheet: assessment decision brief
2. Name the systems and assets - 1,000 words
   - Applications, APIs, hosts, mobile builds, cloud accounts, AI services, third parties
   - Diagram: system boundary map
   - Worksheets: asset register and ownership record
3. Map users, roles, tenants, and critical workflows - 1,100 words
   - Horizontal and vertical authorization, lifecycle states, cross-tenant tests
   - Worksheets: role matrix and workflow inventory

## Part II Describe the test surface

4. Choose coverage by application type - 1,500 words
   - Web, API, mobile, cloud, connected SaaS, and AI-enabled workflows
   - Table: coverage modules and required inputs
5. Select environments, data, and dependencies - 850 words
   - Production versus staging, synthetic data, downstream services, third-party limits
   - Worksheet: environment and data handling plan
6. Define reconnaissance and discovery boundaries - 750 words
   - Known targets, passive discovery, active enumeration, newly discovered assets
   - Worksheet: discovery rules

## Part III Make the engagement lawful and safe

7. Write authorization and rules of engagement - 1,200 words
   - Ownership, written authority, test window, source addresses, prohibited actions, stop conditions
   - Diagram: authorization gate
   - Worksheet: rules of engagement schedule
8. Prepare accounts, access, and monitoring - 850 words
   - Test identities, credentials, MFA, logging, allowlisting, escalation
   - Worksheet: access readiness checklist
9. State exclusions, limits, and unknowns - 700 words
   - Exclusion rationale, rate limits, destructive actions, residual uncertainty
   - Worksheet: exclusion and limitation register

## Part IV Agree on the output

10. Set evidence and reporting requirements - 1,100 words
    - Reproduction, impact, severity, sanitization, report versions, audience
    - Worksheet: finding and report acceptance criteria
11. Define remediation and retesting - 750 words
    - Remediation states, retest scope, evidence, closure rules
    - Worksheet: retest agreement
12. Fix the schedule and responsibilities - 650 words
    - Contacts, communication cadence, incident escalation, delivery dates, RACI
    - Worksheet: engagement operating plan

## Part V Issue the scope

13. Assemble the statement of work - 700 words
    - Scope summary, assumptions, change control, acceptance
    - Template: one-page scope brief
14. Worked example for a multi-tenant SaaS platform - 1,200 words
    - Synthetic support application with browser, API, two tenants, admin role, and AI order lookup
    - Completed example worksheets
