### Wiz (Google Cloud)

Wiz launched AI-SPM (AI Security Posture Management) in November 2023 — described at the time as the first AI-SPM feature inside a CNAPP (cloud-native application protection platform). Features include an AI bill-of-materials (AI-BOM) to surface shadow AI, misconfiguration detection, exposed-endpoint detection, attack-path analysis through Wiz's Security Graph, and AI-specific DSPM and runtime protection. Closed its acquisition by Google Cloud on March 11, 2026 at an announced $32B, with the Wiz brand retained. ([wiz.io/solutions/ai-spm](https://www.wiz.io/solutions/ai-spm))

### CrowdStrike

Falcon AI Detection and Response (AIDR), built on the Pangea acquisition, reached general availability December 15, 2025. It blocks prompt injection and jailbreak attempts and cites internal research spanning "180+ known prompt injection techniques" (vendor claim). CrowdStrike also offers Falcon Cloud Security AI-SPM and the AI Red Team Services covered in our [red teaming guide](/blog/ai-red-teaming-companies/). ([crowdstrike.com](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-announces-general-availability-of-falcon-ai-detection-and-response/))

1. **"I need to find vulnerabilities in our AI system"** → you want a testing vendor, not a GRC platform. See Sthuthi Securities above or go to [AI penetration testing companies](/blog/ai-penetration-testing-companies/) and [AI red teaming companies](/blog/ai-red-teaming-companies/) for the full comparison.

## FAQ

**What's the difference between an "AI security company" and an "AI pentest company"?**
"AI security company" is the broad umbrella — it includes testing firms, runtime protection platforms, and governance/compliance tools. "AI pentest company" is one specific category within it: firms that test your AI systems for vulnerabilities.

**Do I need a GRC platform like Vanta or Drata if I already hire a pentest firm?**
Usually yes, for different reasons — the pentest finds vulnerabilities; the GRC platform documents your ongoing governance program (policies, risk assessments, control evidence) that an ISO 42001 or SOC 2 auditor also wants to see.

**Why do so many "AI security company" lists look so different from each other?**
Because the category is genuinely broad and most lists are written by one of the vendors in it, who naturally emphasize their own category — including this one, which is why we've flagged where we sit in it.

**Is Wiz an AI security company or a cloud security company?**
Both — Wiz is primarily a cloud-native application protection platform (CNAPP) that added AI-specific posture management (AI-SPM) as a module, now under Google Cloud.

**What does "shadow AI" mean, and why do WitnessAI and Aona AI focus on it?**
Shadow AI is employee use of AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, or other apps) outside IT's visibility or approval — a growing risk category distinct from securing your own product's AI features, which is why it gets its own specialist vendors.

**Which of these companies actually test for the OWASP LLM Top 10?**
The testing-category vendors — Sthuthi Securities and the names in our [penetration testing](/blog/ai-penetration-testing-companies/) and [red teaming](/blog/ai-red-teaming-companies/) guides — explicitly map to it. The GRC platforms (Vanta, Drata) reference frameworks like NIST AI RMF for documentation purposes but don't run technical tests themselves.

**Should a startup buy a GRC platform before it has an AI pentest done, or after?**
Usually after, or at least alongside — a pentest produces findings and evidence that make the GRC platform's control documentation meaningful rather than theoretical.
